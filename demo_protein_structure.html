📖 About Protein Folding
Proteins are long chains of amino acids that spontaneously fold into complex 3D shapes. α-helices form spiral structures stabilized by hydrogen bonds between backbone atoms. β-sheets are extended zigzag chains that align side-by-side. Most proteins combine both structures plus random coils, allowing them to perform specific biological functions like catalyzing reactions or carrying oxygen.
