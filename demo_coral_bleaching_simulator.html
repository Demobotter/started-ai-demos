Coral Symbiosis 101

What's happening: Coral polyps host zooxanthellae — photosynthetic algae that give coral its color and up to 90% of its energy through photosynthesis.

The threat: When ocean temps spike 1–2°C above normal, zooxanthellae become toxic to the coral host. The coral expels them (bleaching), losing its energy source and color.

Recovery: If temps cool quickly (weeks), new zooxanthellae can recolonize. But sustained heat → permanent damage and coral death.

Real world: Global marine heat waves are forcing more frequent, more severe bleaching events. Even 1.5°C of ocean warming could destroy 70–90% of world's coral reefs.
