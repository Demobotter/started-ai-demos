How Diceware Works

This tool generates passphrases using real cryptographic randomness (window.crypto.getRandomValues). For each word, we roll 5 virtual d6 dice to create a 5-digit sequence (like 23451), then look it up in the official EFF Diceware word list (7,776 words). That's ~12.9 bits of entropy per word—far stronger than trying to remember "complicated" passwords like "P@ssw0rd2024!"

Why It Works

A 6-word passphrase has ~77.5 bits of entropy (6 × 12.9). A typical GPU cluster would take billions of years to guess it by brute force. Human-created passwords average ~30 bits—much weaker.

Your Passphrase

Roll the dice (or click Refresh) to generate a new passphrase. Each roll is independent and cryptographically random. Write it down, memorize the first 2-3 words if you like, and use the full phrase for high-security accounts (password managers, email, SSH keys, crypto wallets).
