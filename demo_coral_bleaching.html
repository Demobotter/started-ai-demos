🌊 Why Corals Bleach

Zooxanthellae are symbiotic algae living inside coral cells, providing nutrition through photosynthesis. When ocean temperature rises just a few degrees above normal, corals expel their algae as a stress response— leaving behind pale white skeletons. Without their algal partners, corals starve. Many bleached corals die before recovery can begin. Real marine heat waves are pushing reefs past their survival limits.
