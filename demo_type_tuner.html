💡 Typography Guidelines

Letter Spacing: WCAG recommends ≥0.12em for accessibility. Headings can go tighter; body text benefits from 0–0.05em.

Line Height: 1.4–1.6 is optimal for readability. Looser spacing (1.8+) works for display text; tighter (1.2) only for very short lines.

Font Metrics: Baseline alignment, x-height, and ascender/descender space determine perceived balance even when measurements are identical.
