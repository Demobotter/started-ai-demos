🍽️ Fun Fact

Spherification is used in fine dining to create "caviar pearls" of concentrated flavor. A drop of flavored liquid coated in a thin gel membrane bursts when bitten, releasing intense flavor. The membrane forms instantly when sodium alginate (a seaweed extract) droplets meet calcium chloride — the calcium ions cross-link the alginate polymer chains into a hydrogel membrane in milliseconds. The thicker the alginate concentration, the stronger the membrane — but too thick and it becomes rubbery. Master chefs dial in the concentration for perfect texture: thin enough to burst easily, strong enough to hold shape.
