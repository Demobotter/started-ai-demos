How It Works

Breakeven = Fixed Costs ÷ (Selling Price − Variable Cost)

At breakeven, total revenue equals total costs. Below breakeven, you lose money. Above, you profit.

Example: $5,000 fixed costs ÷ ($45 − $15) = 5,000 ÷ 30 = 167 units

Key Insight: Every additional unit sold above breakeven = (Price − Var Cost) in profit. The gap between them is your "contribution margin."
