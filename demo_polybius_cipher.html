How Polybius Works

Ancient Cipher (150 BCE)

Encode: For each letter, find its row (1-5) and column (1-5) in the grid. Combine them as one 2-digit number. String together all digit pairs to make the encoded message.

Decode: Read the encoded numbers in pairs (row, column), then look up the intersection in the grid.

The Key: The grid itself IS the cipher key. Rearrange the letters, and the same plaintext encodes to a completely different number sequence.

Try: Encode "HELLO" → write down the output. Then shuffle the grid and encode "HELLO" again — watch the numbers change entirely!
